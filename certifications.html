Certifications & Compliance

Audited Facilities, Documented Standards

Our three facilities maintain current certifications across labor, social and environmental compliance frameworks recognized by major international retailers.

Social Compliance

WRAP Gold Certification

All three facilities hold WRAP Gold status, certifying compliance with the 12 WRAP Principles on labor, health/safety and environmental practices.

Social Compliance

amfori BSCI

Audited under the amfori Business Social Compliance Initiative Code of Conduct, with reports available to BSCI member buyers.

Social Compliance

Sedex / SMETA

Registered on Sedex with 4-Pillar SMETA audit reports covering labor standards, health & safety, environment and business ethics.

Product Safety

Fabric and finished garment testing for harmful substances, supporting customer chemical-safety requirements.

Quality Management

Environmental

Materials

Global Recycled Standard and Global Organic Textile Standard chain-of-custody available for qualifying fabric programs.

Retailer Programs

Retailer-Specific Audits

Experience hosting customer-specific compliance audits required by major North American and European retail programs.

Our Approach

Compliance as Operating Practice, Not a Checklist

We treat labor standards, workplace safety and environmental management as core to how the factories run day to day — not preparation exercises ahead of a scheduled audit.

Internal social compliance staff conduct quarterly self-audits at each facility, and corrective action plans from any third-party audit finding are tracked to closure with documented evidence.
