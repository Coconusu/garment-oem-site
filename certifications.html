Our Approach

Compliance as Operating Practice, Not a Checklist

We treat labor standards, workplace safety and environmental management as core to how the factories run day to day — not preparation exercises ahead of a scheduled audit.

Internal social compliance staff conduct quarterly self-audits at each facility, and corrective action plans from any third-party audit finding are tracked to closure with documented evidence.
