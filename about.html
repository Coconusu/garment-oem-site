About Ha Bac Garment

Two Decades of Manufacturing Discipline

A Bắc Giang-based, export-focused garment manufacturer built for brands that need consistent quality at scale.

Our Story

Founded in 2002, Built for Export

Công ty Cổ phần May Xuất khẩu Hà Bắc (Ha Bac Export Garment Joint Stock Company) was established in September 2002 in Bắc Giang Province to serve international apparel brands seeking a stable, compliant production base in Vietnam. The company has grown into a two-plant operation employing over 3,000 management staff and skilled workers.

Today we manufacture for major international retailers and fashion brands, with a focus on long-term production partnerships rather than one-off orders. We are ranked among Vietnam's 50 largest garment enterprises and have been recognized as a FAST500 fastest-growing enterprise (2015–2020) and a 2020 Trustworthy Export Enterprise by Vietnam's Ministry of Industry and Trade.

Our management team has overseen full-package export production for nearly two decades, building internal systems so customers can audit our facilities with confidence and verify our compliance with international labor and environmental standards.
