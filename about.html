Our Story

Founded in 2008, Built for Export

VietPhong Garments was founded in Ho Chi Minh City in 2008 to serve international apparel brands seeking a stable, compliant production base outside China. What began as a single 6-line knitwear workshop has grown into a three-facility group spanning Ho Chi Minh City, Binh Duong and Long An provinces.

Today we manufacture for mid-market and premium fashion brands, private-label retailers and sourcing agents across North America, Europe and Australia, with a focus on long-term production partnerships rather than one-off orders.

Our management team has overseen full-package production through multiple retail compliance regimes — from early WRAP certification to current Higg Index environmental reporting — and built internal systems so customers can audit our facilities with confidence.
