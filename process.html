Production Process

From Tech Pack to Shipped Container

A documented, repeatable process designed to give brand partners visibility at every stage.

01

Inquiry & Tech Pack Review

Submit your tech pack, target quantity and timeline via our RFQ form. Our merchandising team reviews construction, fabric and trim requirements and follows up with clarifying questions within 24–48 hours.

02

Sampling & Costing

Proto and fit samples are produced and costed transparently, with line-item breakdowns for fabric, trim, labor and overhead. Typical sample turnaround is 7–10 working days.

03

Material Sourcing & Approval

Fabric and trims are sourced from our network of approved mills. Lab dips, strike-offs and bulk fabric inspection reports are shared for sign-off before cutting begins.

04

Bulk Order Confirmation & PO

Pre-production (PP) sample approval, purchase order confirmation, and production scheduling against our line capacity.

05

Cutting, Sewing & Assembly

Computerized multi-ply cutting followed by modular line sewing, with in-line quality checks at each work station rather than only at the end of the line.

06

Washing, Dyeing & Finishing

Garment dye, denim wash and pressing/finishing as required by style, followed by trimming and thread cleaning.

07

Quality Control & Final Inspection

100% inline inspection plus AQL 2.5 final random inspection. Customer-nominated third-party inspectors (Intertek, SGS, Bureau Veritas) are welcomed for pre-shipment inspection.

08

Packing, Labeling & Shipping

Retail-ready folding, polybagging and carton packing per your packing list, with FOB, CIF or DDP shipment booked through Cat Lai or Cai Mep port.

Quality Control

Inspection Standards

Every order passes through three checkpoints: inline inspection during sewing, end-of-line inspection before finishing, and a final randomized AQL 2.5 inspection prior to packing.

We maintain detailed inspection records per PO and provide inspection reports to customers on request. Customer or third-party inspectors may attend any stage with advance notice.

Inspection Checkpoints

  • •Inline (DHU): Defects-per-hundred-units tracked per line, per hour.
  • •End-of-line: 100% garment check before wash/finish.
  • •Final random (AQL 2.5): Per ISO 2859-1 sampling tables.
  • •Pre-shipment: Customer/third-party inspection accommodated.
