What We Manufacture

Knit Shirts

T-shirts and polo shirts in single jersey, interlock and rib knit fabrications for major retail programs.

  • • Screen print & embroidery
  • • Garment dye available
  • • Quantities by negotiation

Women's Shirts

Woven and knit women's tops in cotton poplin, viscose and blended fabrics for fashion and basics programs.

  • • Pattern grading in-house
  • • Pintuck, pleating, trims
  • • Print & yarn-dye fabric sourcing
  • • Quantities by negotiation

Jackets

Jackets and outerwear with synthetic and insulated fill options, bonded seams and weather-resistant shells.

  • • Synthetic & down insulation
  • • Taped seams, waterproof zips
  • • Hardware & trim sourcing
  • • Quantities by negotiation

Fleece & Sweatshirts

Crewnecks, hoodies and fleece tops in brushed-back and French terry knits for retail basics programs.

  • • Flatlock seaming
  • • Screen print & embroidery
  • • Garment dye available
  • • Quantities by negotiation

Pants, Shorts & Skirts

Long pants, shorts and skirts in woven and stretch-blend fabrics for retail bottoms programs.

  • • Pattern grading in-house
  • • Zippers, rivets, hardware sourcing
  • • Wash & finishing on request
  • • Quantities by negotiation

Children's Wear

Knit and woven children's apparel produced to the same line standards and compliance review as our adult product lines.
