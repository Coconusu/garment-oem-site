Product Categories

What We Manufacture

Knitwear

T-shirts, polos, hoodies, fleece and sweater knits in single jersey, interlock, French terry and rib fabrications.

  • • MOQ: 1,500 pcs/style-color
  • • Cotton, CVC, poly-blends
  • • Screen print & embroidery
  • • Garment dye available

Woven Shirts, Blouses & Dresses

Button-downs, blouses, dresses and lightweight bottoms in cotton poplin, viscose, linen-blends and chiffon.

  • • MOQ: 2,000 pcs/style-color
  • • Pattern grading in-house
  • • Pintuck, pleating, trims
  • • Print & yarn-dye fabric sourcing

Outerwear

Jackets, parkas, vests and coats with down/synthetic fill, bonded and laminated technical shells.

  • • MOQ: 1,000 pcs/style-color
  • • Down & synthetic insulation
  • • Taped seams, waterproof zips
  • • RDS-compliant down sourcing

Activewear & Performance

Leggings, sports bras, training tops and compression wear in moisture-wicking, four-way stretch fabrics.

  • • MOQ: 2,000 pcs/style-color
  • • Flatlock & bonded seaming
  • • Sublimation printing
  • • Recycled performance yarns

Denim

Jeans, jackets and skirts with in-house washing, dyeing and finishing for vintage, raw and stretch denim.

  • • MOQ: 2,500 pcs/style-color
  • • Stone, enzyme & laser wash
  • • Rivets, hardware sourcing
  • • Lower water-use wash recipes

Workwear & Uniforms

Durable workwear, hospitality and corporate uniform programs with reinforced stitching and reflective trims.

  • • MOQ: 1,500 pcs/style-color
  • • Flame-retardant & hi-vis options
  • • Custom branding & name tags
  • • Repeat-program inventory holding
